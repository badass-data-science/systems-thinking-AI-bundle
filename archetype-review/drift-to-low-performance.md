# Review: Drift to Low Performance

Card: [`systems-thinking/archetypes/drift-to-low-performance.md`](../systems-thinking/archetypes/drift-to-low-performance.md)  
Chapters: 15, 8, 14  
Attribution on the card: Peter Senge, The Fifth Discipline (1990), as Eroding Goals; this name from Donella Meadows, Thinking in Systems (2008)

**Summary on the card:** A goal quietly erodes toward whatever performance is being achieved, because lowering the target closes the gap faster than improving performance does.

**Diagnostic question:** Has the goal itself quietly moved down to meet current results, rather than results being pushed up to meet the goal?
