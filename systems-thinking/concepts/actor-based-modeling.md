The field calls this "agent-based modeling." The book renames it "actor-based modeling" to stop the collision with agentic AI: a simulated drone in a formation-keeping model is an actor; a coding assistant taking real actions is an agent. The two rhyme only where many agents answer one another with nobody in charge, and rhyming isn't identity.

Actor-based modeling is explicitly out of scope for the book's toolkit. When a reader's problem passes the swarm test and needs simulation, actor-based modeling (for example in AnyLogic) is the next place to look.
