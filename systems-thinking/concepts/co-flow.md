title: Co-flow
summary: A second stock, with its own pipes, that shadows a main stock to track one attribute of what it holds, so that dividing the shadow by the main stock gives that attribute's share.
chapters: [16]
links:
  part-of: [stock-and-flow-diagram]
  related: [delay]
---

Whenever material enters or leaves the main stock, the attribute's portion of it enters or leaves the shadow stock too. Example: the main stock is material in transit; the co-flow is *rushed* material in transit. Rushed ÷ total is the rushed share, which can drive an auxiliary such as corner-cutting at the receiving end, lagging the rush by the transit time.
