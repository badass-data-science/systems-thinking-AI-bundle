Completes the boundary work begun with CATWOE. The audience's worldview is part of the system being modeled, whether or not the model accounts for it (the soft-systems lesson). Ulrich's critical systems heuristics treat questioning a boundary's built-in value judgments, including who it leaves out, as the core of systems practice.

Practical form: for each stakeholder group, mark *consulted while drawing*, *informed afterward*, or *not yet consulted*, and plan the next conversation for the last group.
