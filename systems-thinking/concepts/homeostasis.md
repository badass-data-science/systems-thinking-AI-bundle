The oldest balancing loop the cybernetics tradition formalized. Use the thermostat carefully as a metaphor: when a non-physical loop (attention management, budget control) works "like a thermostat," it means the two loops are wired the same way (state, goal, gap, correction), not that they share anything else.

"Stability" carries a positive connotation that can do unearned work in an argument. A homeostatic loop defending a bad goal actively defeats needed change.
