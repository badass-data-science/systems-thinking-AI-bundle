Von Bertalanffy, a biologist, noticed that biology, engineering and the social sciences kept reinventing the same structural ideas (open systems, hierarchy, equifinality, meaning that different starting points can converge on the same outcome) without talking to each other, and tried to build one unifying vocabulary.

Its gift is vocabulary: "system," "boundary," "elements, interconnections, purpose" are worth naming carefully. Its trap is altitude. Vocabulary alone doesn't say what to build, measure or fix, and analysis that stays at that level of abstraction changes nothing. The book teaches systems *thinking* as the practiced habit of applying the vocabulary to a specific mess.
