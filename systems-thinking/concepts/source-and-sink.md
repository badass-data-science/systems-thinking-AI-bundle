title: Source and sink (cloud)
summary: The cloud symbol at the open end of a pipe in a stock-and-flow diagram, marking where material comes from or goes to outside the model's boundary, untracked.
chapters: [16]
links:
  part-of: [stock-and-flow-diagram]
  related: [system-boundary]
---

A pipe carries everything that enters it, so losses need their own drain: a 90% yield means a 10% loss pipe ending in a cloud. The cloud is a boundary decision drawn into the diagram itself.
