title: Anchoring
summary: The pull an early number, even an arbitrary first-draft guess, keeps exerting over every later estimate long after better information exists.
chapters: [18]
attribution: Amos Tversky and Daniel Kahneman; see Kahneman, Thinking, Fast and Slow (2011)
links:
  related: [validate-a-model]

---

The guessed 0.5 for quality lost to corner-cutting is a first-draft number of exactly this kind, and it is the one the recommendation depends on. **Counter-practice**: swing it, and have someone estimate it independently before they see yours.
