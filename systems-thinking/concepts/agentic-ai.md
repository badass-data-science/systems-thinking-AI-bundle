Agentic AI inherits from every systems tradition. It is cybernetic (feedback keeping a process on target); it fits the general-systems vocabulary of elements, interconnections and purpose; its usage, trust and scrutiny form stocks, flows and loops that system dynamics can model (Ch. 20); and it inherits Checkland's lesson that stakeholders will disagree about what the problem was.

Most agentic AI fails the swarm test because someone is in charge of it. It becomes a swarm only when many independently built agents act on one another's outputs with nobody coordinating.
