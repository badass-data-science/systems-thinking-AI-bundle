Cybernetics, general systems theory and system dynamics broadly assume the problem is defined well enough to model. Checkland worked with organizations where people couldn't agree on what the problem was, and built a method for that more common case: model the contestation first.

One SSM device is the **rich picture**: a large, deliberately informal sketch built live by all the parties in one room, showing each stakeholder's view of what is going on (worries, relationships, disagreements), drawn with cartoons and speech bubbles rather than boxes and arrows. The goal is agreement on the *system* even while blame is still disputed.

CATWOE (Ch. 4) comes from SSM.
