Lorenz found that a simplified weather model produced wildly different long-term forecasts from starting points differing by a fraction of a percent. The lesson for systems thinking: even a perfectly drawn structure can be unpredictable far enough out. Drawing the structure better doesn't remove that limit.

Chaos and complexity are different fields. Chaos concerns unpredictability in simple, fully specified systems; complexity concerns coordinated behavior emerging from many interacting actors. The book does not teach chaos mathematics.
