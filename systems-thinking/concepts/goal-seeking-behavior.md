title: Goal-seeking behavior
summary: "The signature curve of a balancing loop without significant delay: the state approaches its goal quickly at first, then more and more gently, because the correction is sized to a shrinking gap."
chapters: [8]
links:
  related: [balancing-loop, oscillation-and-overshoot]
---

On a graph of state over time: steep at the start, flattening as it nears a level line, like a car easing up to a stop sign. It's as recognizable as a reinforcing loop's flat-then-steep curve.

When a balancing loop overshoots instead, swinging past its goal and back, a delay is almost always involved (see oscillation and overshoot).
