type: concept
title: Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG)
aliases: [RAG, grounding]
summary: An AI system fetching facts from a source before answering, rather than answering from what it learned in training; a knowledge graph is one structured source for it.
chapters: [23]
attribution: Patrick Lewis et al., "Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Knowledge-Intensive NLP Tasks" (2020)
links:
  related: [knowledge-graph, graphs-not-just-chats]
series: [knowledge-representation, ai-delegation]
---

Retrieval from a structured graph makes answers reproducible and traceable in a way that retrieval from prose chunks does not. Either way, retrieval doesn't make the agent right; it makes its factual claims cheaper to check.
