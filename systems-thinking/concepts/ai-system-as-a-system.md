Not a metaphor. Accumulated trust is a stock (it survives the snapshot test); usage volume and scrutiny per output are flows. The agent's loops sit *inside* the systems a technical professional is already responsible for, alongside staffing, budgets and allocation rules, and they produce the same failure modes: delayed information, a proxy standing in for a goal, a capability atrophying from disuse. Those structures don't care whether the loop passes through a person, a spreadsheet or a language model.

Several phenomena discussed as exotic AI concerns turn out to be known archetypes in new vocabulary: specification gaming is Fixes That Fail; growing dependence on an agent is Shifting the Burden to the Intervenor.
