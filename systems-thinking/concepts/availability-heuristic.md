---
id: availability-heuristic
type: concept
title: Availability heuristic
summary: Overweighting whatever example comes to mind most easily, whether or not it's typical.
chapters: [18]
attribution: Amos Tversky and Daniel Kahneman; see Kahneman, Thinking, Fast and Slow (2011)
links:
  related: [validate-a-model]
series: [cognitive-bias]
---

A modeler who just watched one dramatic quality failure sets the quality-loss parameter by that batch. **Counter-practice**: ask the record, not your memory, how often the event has happened before choosing the number.
