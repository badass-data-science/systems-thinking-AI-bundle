The name comes from the Greek for "helmsman." Wiener's insight was that a thermostat, a torpedo's guidance system and a nervous system share one mathematical question: how does a system use information about how far off target it is to get back on target? The canonical example is a thermostat: measure temperature, compare it with a setpoint, apply heat in proportion to the gap.

Cybernetics is the root of the balancing loop (Ch. 8). Its blind spot, noted early in the book, is the system engineered to hold steady meeting a world in which someone wants it to move, or moves its setpoint on purpose.

A drone swarm is cybernetics multiplied: each unit runs a small balancing loop toward its neighbors, whose positions keep moving because they are steering toward it in turn. The formation emerges without any unit being given it.
