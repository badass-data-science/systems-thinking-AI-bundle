Marked on a CLD with a pair of hash marks across the link, ideally labeled with its kind.

- **Information delay**: the cause happened, but whoever needs to know doesn't yet (an investigation that becomes visible only when it goes public; a case count reported days after infection).
- **Material delay**: everyone knows, but the state of the world hasn't caught up (accumulated attention decaying on its own slow clock; material in a shipping pipeline; a new hire's training showing up in performance weeks later).

**A delay is a stock in disguise.** A material delay is something sitting in a pipeline and lasts as long as that stock takes to drain. An information delay is a stock of belief, catching up with the real level only gradually. A model that has to run numerically turns each delay into a stock (Ch. 16).

**A delay doesn't just slow a loop; it changes its behavior.** It can turn a well-tuned balancing loop into an oscillator, and hide a reinforcing loop's overshoot until it has already gone too far.
