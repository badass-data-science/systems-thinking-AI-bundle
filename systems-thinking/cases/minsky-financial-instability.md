## Situation

Hyman Minsky argued through the 1970s and 1980s that financial stability itself encourages the over-leveraging that ends it. The argument was mostly ignored until 2008, when "Minsky Moment" became a stock phrase.

## Analysis

A reinforcing loop: rising asset prices justify more borrowing, more borrowing bids prices higher. Every participant's step is individually rational; the aggregate removes the slack the system needs to absorb a shock. The stopping signal (a shock exposing the leverage) arrives late, so the spiral overshoots well past what was sustainable.

## Lesson

The same structures recur across fields under different vocabulary. An economist found this one independently, without the term "systems archetype." Look for a reinforcing loop wherever stability is said to justify taking on more risk.
