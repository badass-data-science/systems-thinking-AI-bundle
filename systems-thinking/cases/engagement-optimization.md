## Situation

Online platforms in the 2000s and 2010s optimized for engagement proxies. Nobody designed an outrage-amplification machine; many individually reasonable optimization decisions, compounding, produced one.

## Analysis

Specification gaming at organizational scale: the proxy went up reliably and immediately, while the goal it stood in for degraded somewhere unmeasured. Where the damage eventually dragged the metric down too, the structure became Fixes That Fail. The aggregate shape, rational at every step and ruinous in total, is the one Escalation names.

## Lesson

A single optimized proxy is a loop with an unmeasured side. Ask what the proxy stands in for and where degradation of that would show up, before the metric itself turns.
