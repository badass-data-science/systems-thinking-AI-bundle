## Situation

Past a few hundred staff, a fixed training team is spread too thin and results suffer. Past a few hundred more, housing fills and the overflow site is forty minutes away. And every success adds, after a delay, to outside scrutiny, until working there carries real personal risk.

## Analysis

One reinforcing growth loop, three balancing loops of different kinds. More trainers cost money; more housing costs money and most of a year; scrutiny can't be bought off at all. This period's budget covers one of the first two. Hire trainers and housing fills sooner; build housing and more successes bring scrutiny sooner.

## Lesson

Decide which unattractiveness to accept on purpose: crowded housing rather than undertrained staff, because one costs comfort and the other costs results. Not choosing hands the decision to whichever limit binds first.
