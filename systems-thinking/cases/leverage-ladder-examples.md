## Situation

The same organization has a Fixes That Fail structure (overtime and burnout) and a Success to the Successful structure (training budget following early wins).

## Analysis

Overtime pay is a parameter: visible, easy, touching nothing about why success slipped. Changing the allocation rule so budget no longer purely follows success is a rule change that removes the mechanism manufacturing the advantage. Redefining success in quarterly reviews away from this quarter's raw success rate, toward a measure that counts burnout incurred and teams starved, is a paradigm move: it acts on the mental model under both archetypes at once. Power rises up the ladder while reach (what can be done Monday morning) falls; the curves cross near the middle rung.

## Lesson

Name both axes. The rule change is often the best combination of power and reach; the paradigm change is worth starting even if it can't be finished soon.
