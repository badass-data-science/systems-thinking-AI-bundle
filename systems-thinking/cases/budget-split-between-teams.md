## Situation

Two newly formed teams share a fixed quarterly training budget. Team A's first project succeeds, partly on skill and partly on luck. The win earns it a bigger share; the bigger share improves its next result; and so on.

## Analysis

The allocation rule (more budget to recent winners) is the reinforcing mechanism. Team B, no less capable at the outset by any measure taken, is starved of the training that would let it catch up. The boardroom story is "A was always better," which flatters the original allocation call and requires nobody to revisit anything.

## Lesson

Change the rule: allocate by what each team needs to reach a shared goal, guarantee a floor, or give each its own budget.
