## Situation

Case counts oscillated during the pandemic. There was a delay between infection and a positive test, and a further delay between a positive test and a public case count. Supply chains also seized, exposing boundaries that had been drawn around whatever data existed rather than around what mattered.

## Analysis

A balancing loop (policy responding to case counts) acting on information that lagged the real state. Each response was sized to where the system had been. React hard enough to a late signal and the correction overshoots, then the counter-correction overshoots too.

## Lesson

No new structure was needed to explain it; a balancing loop with information delays is enough. When feedback is late, react less hard, or shorten the delay.
