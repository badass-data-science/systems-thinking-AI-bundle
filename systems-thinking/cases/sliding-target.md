## Situation

No single decision lowered the target. After each miss, someone proposed being realistic given current conditions, and the target moved.

## Analysis

Improving performance takes time (a delay); lowering the target takes a meeting. The faster loop wins, so the gap closes by moving the goal.

## Lesson

Anchor the target outside the system's own recent results (customer need, a competitor's level, the organization's own good year), and make any change to it an explicit decision.
