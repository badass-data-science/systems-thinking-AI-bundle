## Situation

Six quarters of what felt like exponential applicant growth visibly bend.

## Analysis

The region has a finite number of people who would ever consider the job: the carrying capacity. As applicants approach it, a balancing loop takes over from the reinforcing reputation loop. Nothing broke.

## Lesson

Bigger recruiting drives or signing bonuses mostly make the limit push back harder. Name the limit and ask whether it can be relaxed (recruit in a new region, change who the job appeals to) or must be planned around.
