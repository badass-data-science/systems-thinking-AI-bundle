## Situation

A recurring processing fault can be reliably diagnosed by only one person. Calling them is quick and always works.

## Analysis

The intervenor is available on demand, so there is every short-term incentive to keep calling them rather than invest in a second capable person. Within a year nobody else can even describe the fault properly.

## Lesson

The same shape appears, unchanged, when the intervenor is an AI agent: the model quietly becomes the only thing in the building that can still do the job (Ch. 20). Train the second person while the first is still around, and register the dependency as a risk.
