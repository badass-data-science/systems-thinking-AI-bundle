## Situation

The reinforcing reputation loop keeps filling the bench. The balancing training-capacity loop acts through a delay: a new hire's thin training becomes visible as sloppy execution only once they start working, weeks later.

## Analysis

During the delay, hiring continues well past what the training staff can handle. When sloppiness finally reaches success rates, the brake bites late and hard: an overshoot, then a fall back toward what the trainers can sustain.

## Lesson

A spiral meeting its limit (Limits to Growth) produces a smooth leveling-off only if the limit's feedback is prompt. With a delay, expect overshoot and fall; watch leading indicators of the limit (training hours per new hire) rather than its lagging effect (execution quality).
