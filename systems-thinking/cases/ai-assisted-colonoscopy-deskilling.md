## Situation

Clinicians became used to an AI tool that flags polyps during colonoscopy, then performed some procedures without it.

## Analysis

Their detection of precancerous growths in standard (non-AI) procedures fell from about 28% to about 22%. The study is observational, not proof of cause, but the shape matches: the specialist's own pattern recognition gets less exercise the more reliably the tool catches the case first.

## Lesson

Capability erosion from an AI intervenor is not hypothetical. Measure unaided performance periodically wherever a tool has taken over a skilled judgment.
