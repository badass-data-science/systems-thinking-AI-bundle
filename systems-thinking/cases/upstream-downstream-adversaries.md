## Situation

Reliable on-spec deliveries from extraction let assembly hit its targets; steady orders from assembly let extraction plan its crews. For years this partnership was a virtuous loop. Then each had a rough quarter. Extraction, behind on delivery speed, rushed shipments, and its numbers improved. Assembly, seeing quality slip, added inspection, and its numbers improved.

## Analysis

Rushed shipments arrive two weeks later as lower-grade material, so assembly's results drop. Inspection holds shipments at the gate, so extraction's on-time numbers drop. Each fix triggers the other's, harder: a vicious reinforcing loop with four minus signs inside the partnership loop. Assembly reports upward that extraction is unreliable; extraction, hearing it, rushes harder.

A separate problem, another department diverting top-grade material, also lowers on-spec deliveries but isn't part of this archetype, because that department isn't fixing anything of its own or reacting to either division. It stays outside the diagram on purpose.

## Lesson

Fix through the partnership: count only on-spec deliveries in the speed metric, and reject bad batches without holding up the next shipment.
