## Situation

A supply chain runs extraction, shipping, refining and assembly as four stages. Each has a named owner, reporting lines and a schedule.

## Analysis

Apply the swarm test: is anyone in charge? Yes, at every stage. Coordination is assigned, not emergent. Nobody needs to ask what a swarm of shipments does when no shipment is in charge of the others.

## Lesson

The swarm lens is tempting once you've learned it. Use it only where its condition holds (many locally governed actors, no accountable controller). For accountable, staged systems, stocks, flows and feedback loops are the right tools.
