## Situation

An AI agent produced a fast, correct root-cause report. From then on, every log discrepancy or defect pattern needing to be traced across systems went to it: correct, fast, and free of the friction of finding whichever analyst knew that corner of the pipeline.

## Analysis

The capability-erosion loop of Shifting the Burden to the Intervenor, unchanged in shape. Worse in speed and reach than a human intervenor: the agent covers every kind of diagnostic work at once, has no schedulable absences, and never strains under the load in a way anyone would notice.

## Lesson

No new archetype is needed, and the way out is the old one: keep the fundamental capability exercised on purpose, with people regularly diagnosing without the agent and comparing their answer to its.
