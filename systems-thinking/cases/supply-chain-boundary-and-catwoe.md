## Situation

Four stocks (ore in ground, ore in transit, refined material on hand, finished-unit stockpile) joined by three flows (mining rate, refining rate, assembly rate). Excluded on purpose: the mining region's geopolitical stability, and what happens after the finished units are deployed.

## Analysis

The geopolitical exclusion is re-nominated for inclusion every quarter by whoever owns the latest upstream failure. The case for it isn't weak, but those debates re-argue the box rather than redraw it. CATWOE's Transformation (ore in, stockpile out) is clean; the Worldview underneath, that readiness matters more than quality, had never been said aloud, though it was built into the order of the stages.

## Lesson

A good boundary is the smallest one that still holds the structure producing the problem, and CATWOE's Worldview letter is where unstated priorities surface.
