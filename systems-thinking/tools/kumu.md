Reach for it when a CLD has more nodes than fit legibly on a page, needs editing by a distributed team, or has more loops than a human eye can be trusted to find. It doesn't simulate stocks and flows or store facts for querying.

Its loop detection checks your diagram; it doesn't replace the trace-and-count method or the read-it-out-loud test.
