title: Diffuse resistance means an unnamed balancing loop
summary: When a change effort meets small, reasonable resistance from every direction and nobody is visibly opposing it, look for a balancing loop defending a goal the change never addressed.
chapters: [8]
when: A change "can't get traction"; each obstacle is locally reasonable (a capped budget here, a stretched timeline there); no one is sabotaging it.
then: Find the reference point the system is defending (often inherited, like "never exceed last year's spend") and change it explicitly. Moving the state without moving the goal invites the loop to push back.
links:
  uses: [find-the-unwritten-goal]
  related: [balancing-loop, reference-point]
series: [balancing-dynamics]
---

Example: an unstated goal that training spend must not exceed last year's, regardless of headcount, defeats a hiring push through a dozen small decisions. Each one looks reasonable from its own seat. Naming the loop turns a fight with fresh symptoms every quarter into an honest argument about the goal itself.
