---
id: plus-into-a-stock
type: heuristic
title: A plus sign into a stock means "adds to"
summary: On an arrow from a flow into a stock, + means the flow adds to the stock, not that the two move together; a stock keeps rising while its net inflow is positive, even as that inflow shrinks.
chapters: [10, 16]
when: Reading or explaining any CLD arrow that ends at an accumulation.
then: Say "adds to" rather than "rises with," and don't predict that the stock falls just because its inflow is falling.
links:
  related: [stock, flow, causal-loop-diagram]
---

It's the one place a CLD's notation asks more of the reader than it says. Translating the CLD into stocks and flows (Ch. 16) makes it explicit.
