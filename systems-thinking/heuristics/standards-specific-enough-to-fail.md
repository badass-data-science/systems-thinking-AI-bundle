id: standards-specific-enough-to-fail
type: heuristic
title: Write standards specific enough to fail
summary: A check can only catch what its standard is specific enough to name, so a vague description ("a 2x2-plus-center diagram") will pass every wrong arrangement.
chapters: [24, 21]
when: Writing an acceptance criterion, a figure description, a test, or a review checklist.
then: Name the parts and their positions or values, so that a wrong result contradicts the standard.
links:
  related: [every-check-has-a-boundary-and-a-kind]
series: [model-validation]
---

The book's Cynefin figure had its four domains in the wrong quadrants from the first draft and passed every review, because its description never said which domain went where.
