---
id: exponential-looks-flat
type: heuristic
title: Act while the curve still looks flat
summary: If you have traced a reinforcing loop, decide on intervention while its output still looks boring, because compounding is invisible to the eye until several rounds have run.
chapters: [7]
when: A quantity driven by a reinforcing loop is growing slowly in absolute terms, and someone proposes waiting until it "really takes off."
then: Look at the ratio between rounds, not the absolute change. A steady multiplier means the steep part is already scheduled. Decide now whether to dampen or feed it.
links:
  related: [snowballing, reinforcing-loop]
series: [reinforcing-dynamics]
---

Absolute changes of 20 and then 60 look like noise; the same multiplier then gives 180, 540. Waiting for visible proof means waiting until the leverage of early action is gone. The heuristic applies to collapse as much as growth.
