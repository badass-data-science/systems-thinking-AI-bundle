---
id: change-the-comparison
type: heuristic
title: End an escalation by changing what's compared
summary: When two parties are locked in a relative-position race, change the structure of the comparison (a verifiable shared limit, a new dimension to compete on) rather than asking one side to stop first.
chapters: [12, 19]
when: Two parties keep matching and raising each other's moves and both are worse off.
then: Look for a rule that binds both sides verifiably, or a differentiator that shifts what the comparison measures. Consider unilateral standing down only if the other side is driven by threat rather than by a reward that persists either way.
links:
  counters: [escalation]
  related: [leverage-points]
series: [escalation, intervention-leverage]
---

A plea to one participant to feel differently is a low-leverage intervention on an unchanged structure. A new rule, or better a new thing to compare, is mid-to-high leverage.
