---
id: name-the-flow
type: heuristic
title: When a stock is changing, name the flow
summary: A sentence like "our headcount is dropping" hides which flow is responsible, and the inflow and outflow readings call for different fixes.
chapters: [4]
when: Someone reports that a level (headcount, inventory, backlog, cash) is rising or falling.
then: Split the change into inflow and outflow and ask which one moved. Fix that flow.
links:
  uses: [stock, flow]
---

"We're losing people too fast" (outflow: reduce attrition) and "we never hired enough" (inflow: increase recruitment) produce the same plain-English sentence and point at different levers. A level changes only through its flows, so any statement about a changing level is a claim about net flow in disguise.
