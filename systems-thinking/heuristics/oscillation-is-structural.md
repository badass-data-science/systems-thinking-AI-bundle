title: Don't blame people for oscillation
summary: Repeated overshoot under delayed feedback is the expected behavior of the structure, so look for the delay before looking for the person who "should have acted sooner."
chapters: [9, 5, 18]
when: A postmortem on a boom-bust or overshoot names a person who reacted too late or too hard.
then: Ask what that person knew and when. If the information arrived late, the cause is the delay, and replacing the person won't change the swings.
links:
  related: [oscillation-and-overshoot, delay]
---

The tempo couldn't have been reduced sooner, because the information that would have justified reducing it hadn't arrived. The person didn't fail; the delay did what delays do.
