---
id: past-performance-may-be-allocation
type: heuristic
title: Past performance may be a record of past allocation
summary: When resources go to whoever performed best last round, part of each party's track record was produced by the allocation rule itself, not by merit.
chapters: [15]
when: Someone justifies the next resource split by past results.
then: Ask whether the past results were themselves shaped by past allocations from the same pool. If so, discount them, or change the rule (needs-based split, a floor, separate pools).
links:
  diagnoses: [success-to-the-successful]
series: [competing-for-shared-resources]
---

"They were always better" is a comfortable story because it requires nobody to revisit anything. A Success to the Successful structure can manufacture that appearance from an arbitrary early edge.
