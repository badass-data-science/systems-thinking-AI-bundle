---
id: exercise-the-capability
type: heuristic
title: Keep the capability exercised on purpose
summary: When a tool or agent reliably does a skilled task, schedule people to do it without the tool and compare their answer with the tool's, so the human capability doesn't quietly atrophy.
chapters: [20, 11]
when: An AI tool or a single expert has become the default way a diagnostic or judgment task gets done.
then: Set a regular cadence of unaided work, compare human answers against the tool's (not the other way round), and track whether people can still do it.
links:
  counters: [shifting-the-burden-to-the-intervenor]
series: [ai-delegation]
---

An AI intervenor has no vacation days and never visibly strains, so nothing prompts anyone to ask whether a second capable person still exists. An observational 2025 study of colonoscopy in four Polish endoscopy centers found clinicians' adenoma detection rate in standard procedures fell from about 28% to about 22% after they became used to an AI polyp-detection tool; not proof of cause, but the same shape.
