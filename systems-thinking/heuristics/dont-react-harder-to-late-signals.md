type: heuristic
title: Don't react harder to a late signal
summary: If a system oscillates because its feedback is delayed, reacting faster or more forcefully to the same late signal enlarges the swings.
chapters: [9]
when: After an overshoot, the instinct in the room is to respond to the next signal with more urgency.
then: Check whether the delay is structural. If it is, shorten it, make corrections smaller and wait, or widen the acceptable band.
links:
  related: [damp-an-oscillation, oscillation-and-overshoot]
series: [delay]
---

An outside authority's investigation timeline won't shorten because you pay closer attention to it. A bigger correction applied to information that is still weeks old just overshoots further, however satisfying it feels in the room.
