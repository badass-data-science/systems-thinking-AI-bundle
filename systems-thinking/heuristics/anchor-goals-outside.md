---
id: anchor-goals-outside
type: heuristic
title: Anchor goals outside the system's own results
summary: Tie a target to something the system's recent performance can't move (customer need, a competitor's level, your own best year), so misses stay gaps instead of becoming the new target.
chapters: [15, 14]
when: A target is being revised after a miss.
then: Ask whether the revision is a deliberate reconsideration of what you want or an adjustment to what you're getting. If the latter, keep the target and treat the miss as a gap.
links:
  counters: [drift-to-low-performance]
  related: [reference-point]
series: [balancing-dynamics]
---

Lowering a target closes the gap faster than improving performance does, so the eroding loop wins by default unless the goal is anchored somewhere it can't drift.
