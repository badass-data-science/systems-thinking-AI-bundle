title: When something gets past a check, fix the check
summary: When an error slips past a check, ask whether it fell outside the check's boundary (where it looked) or outside its kind (what it could notice), and change the check, not just the bug.
chapters: [24, 4, 19]
when: A review, test, audit, script or AI pass declared work clean, and an error turned up later.
then: "Classify the miss: outside the boundary (widen what the check covers, ideally from a list generated by the system, not typed by hand) or outside the kind (add a different kind of check: run the code, look up the source, compare with the literature, compare with a specific written standard)."
links:
  related: [checking-loop, audit-your-checks]
series: [model-validation]
---

Fixing the bug corrects one error and leaves the sensor as it was. Fixing the check changes what the loop can see, which is a higher rung on the leverage ladder. And no check is trustworthy just because it's a check, whoever runs it, a review included: before acting on any claim, go back to the source it depends on.
