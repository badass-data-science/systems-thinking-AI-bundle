Bad math gets corrected and forgotten. Decades of argument are what happen when a sound model's conclusions threaten its audience, and the audience finds it easier to dispute where the boundary was drawn, which loops were included, and which parameters are assumptions. Those choices are value-laden decisions dressed as technical ones, which is why they're the natural target.

The 1972 *Limits to Growth* study is the field's largest example: its critics mostly argued about boundary and parameter choices, not the mathematics.
