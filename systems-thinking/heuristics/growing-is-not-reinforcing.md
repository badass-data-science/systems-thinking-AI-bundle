type: heuristic
title: Growth isn't proof of a reinforcing loop
summary: Before crediting a growth spiral to a loop, check that each round's output is what drives the next round's input, not an outside event moving the same way.
chapters: [7]
when: Something is growing and you have a loop diagram that would explain it.
then: Test self-causation. Would the growth continue if the outside conditions reversed? If a competitor's bad quarter or a market-wide tailwind could explain it, the loop may not be what's driving it.
links:
  related: [reinforcing-loop]
---

"Everything is growing" and "this loop is reinforcing" are different claims. A comparative boost from a rival's bad quarter looks like the reputation loop working, but it stops when the rival recovers, with nothing in your own loop having changed. Correlation with the loop's direction isn't the loop.
