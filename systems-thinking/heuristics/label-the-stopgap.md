title: Label a stopgap as a stopgap
summary: A low-leverage fix is fine as a deliberate bridge while a structural fix is built, as long as everyone knows which job it's doing and what it's buying time for.
chapters: [19, 11]
when: A quick, low-leverage fix is about to be adopted.
then: Write down whether it's the fix or a stopgap, and if a stopgap, what structural change it's buying time for and when that change is due.
links:
  counters: [fixes-that-fail, shifting-the-burden]
  related: [rank-interventions]
series: [intervention-leverage]
---

Overtime that covers a rough month while training capacity is built is a defensible use of a weak lever. Overtime as the standing answer is Fixes That Fail. The difference is mostly whether anyone remembered which job it was doing.
