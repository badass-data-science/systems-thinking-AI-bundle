title: When growth stalls, look at the limit, not the engine
summary: Slowing growth against a real constraint is the system behaving as designed; pushing harder on the growth engine mostly makes the constraint push back harder.
chapters: [13, 14]
when: Growth is slowing and the proposed response is more effort, bigger incentives, or a search for what broke.
then: Name the constraint. Remove or relax it if it can be removed; plan around it if it can't. Check whether it's really fixed or a standing decision not to invest.
links:
  related: [limits-to-growth, classify-a-growth-stall]
series: [limits-and-capacity]
---

The tempting misread is "we broke something," which sends people hunting for a component to repair, usually with a quick fix. Nothing broke; a balancing loop that was always there is now dominant.
