type: heuristic
title: Quantify early to surface disagreement, not for precision
summary: Writing an explicit flow equation, even with guessed numbers, forces disagreements that a qualitative diagram lets stay vague, and those are cheapest to resolve on paper.
chapters: [16]
when: A team agrees on a CLD and nobody has committed to numbers.
then: Have each person write the flow equations they believe in. Compare them before running anything.
links:
  related: [hand-iterate-a-model, stock-and-flow-diagram]
series: [model-validation]
---

Two colleagues who nodded along at the same diagram often disagree sharply once each must write a refining-rate equation. Better to discover that now than after a real shortfall.
