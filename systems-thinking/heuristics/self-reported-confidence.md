---
id: self-reported-confidence
type: heuristic
title: Self-reported confidence is not evidence
summary: A confidence signal generated by the system being trusted (a badge, a score, an assertion of certainty) tells you how the output is phrased, not whether it is right or addresses the right question.
chapters: [20, 1, 21]
when: An AI agent's output arrives with its own confidence marker, or a summary that grades itself.
then: Ignore the marker when deciding how much to check. Set scrutiny by the decision's domain and stakes, and check against an independent source.
links:
  related: [ai-trust-loop, fluency-is-not-correctness]
series: [ai-delegation]
mnemonic: "The Q4 report's green '✓ Confidently Diagnosed' badge, which the report had awarded itself."
---

An audience with no independent way to check the confidence will tend to accept it, which discourages exactly the scrutiny that would have caught a gap.
